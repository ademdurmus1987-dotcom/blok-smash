The central 70% of the width is completely empty from top to bottom: no objects at all there. Only small decorations hugging the far left and right edges. Soft gentle depth of field, low detail, low contrast in the center so game pieces stand out. No characters, no animals, no text, no logo, no platform, no cannon, no ship in the center, no crown, no purple and gold."

1) "Tropical morning: a palm leaf at the left and right edges, a few seashells in the bottom corners."
